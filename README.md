# Aggressive Long Short Equity Portfolio

## Overview
This project designs an aggressively managed equity portfolio targeting maximum total return over a one year horizon. The strategy uses both long and short publicly traded stocks with leveraged exposure to simulate a professional active fund management approach.

## Objective
The goal is to construct a high conviction portfolio that seeks outsized returns while maintaining disciplined risk management. The portfolio assumes high volatility tolerance and focuses on capital growth rather than income or preservation.

## Strategy Framework
The portfolio is built using several core components:

- Market and macroeconomic analysis  
- Sector and thematic allocation  
- Individual stock selection  
- Long and short positioning  
- Position sizing and exposure management  
- Risk control through stop losses and rebalancing  

## Portfolio Structure
Total exposure: 200000 USD  
Capital: 100000 USD  
Leverage: 100000 USD  

The strategy allocates capital across high growth sectors while shorting weaker industries expected to underperform.

### Long Positions
Focus areas include:
- AI infrastructure and semiconductors  
- Cloud and digital platforms  
- Industrial automation  
- Healthcare innovation  
- Energy producers

### Short Positions
Target areas include:
- Overvalued software companies  
- Defensive low growth consumer sectors

## Risk Management
- Maximum position sizing limits  
- Defined stop loss levels  
- Sector diversification  
- Periodic rebalancing reviews  

The portfolio prioritizes aggressive growth while preventing uncontrolled downside risk.

## Tactical Management
Positions are reviewed monthly with full portfolio rebalancing conducted quarterly. Allocation adjustments are triggered by earnings revisions, macroeconomic changes, and sector rotation signals.

## Educational Purpose
This repository demonstrates how professional portfolio managers design and manage a leveraged long short equity strategy using real world financial reasoning.
